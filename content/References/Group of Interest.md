Group of Interest (GoI) is a term used by the [[SCP Foundation]] to describe a group that knows of the [[Anomaly|anomalous]] and may potentially be interacting with the anomalous. This can range from innocent groups that simply wish to live alongside the anomalous peacefully, to doomsday cults that wish to see the world turned to ash in the name of a "God".
