Within the [[SCP Foundation]]’s terminology, **ontokinetic** (or **ontokinesis**) is a clinical term used to describe the ability to manipulate or “bend” reality. In other words, an ontokinetic entity can alter the underlying fabric of existence through [[Anomaly|anomalous]] means. Effectively, it exhibits reality-bending powers.

This term is generally used interchangeably with other labels for reality-altering phenomena, and it highlights an [[Anomaly|anomaly]]’s capacity to change what is considered normal, often in ways that defy conventional scientific explanation. Anomalies that are able to bend reality are generally known as Reality Benders
