Within the SCP Foundation’s terminology, **ontokinetic** (or **ontokinesis**) is a clinical term used to describe the ability to manipulate or “bend” reality. In other words, an ontokinetic entity can alter the underlying fabric of existence through anomalous means—effectively, it exhibits reality-bending powers.

This term is generally used interchangeably with other labels for reality-altering phenomena, and it highlights an anomaly’s capacity to change what is considered normal, often in ways that defy conventional scientific explanation.
