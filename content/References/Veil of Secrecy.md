The Veil of Secrecy is a term the [[SCP Foundation]] uses to describe the effort of keeping the world in the dark concerning the [[Anomaly|anomalous]] and [[Group of Interest|Groups of Interest]].

The Foundation sees this as a necessary evil so mankind can live and go on with their daily lives without fear, mistrust, and doubting their personal beliefs, and to maintain human independence from extraterrestrial, extradimensional, and other extranormal influence.

The Foundation accomplishes this by using censorship, [[Amnestics|amnestics]], blackmail, and more.

If the Foundation is forced to make knowledge of the anomalous and Groups of Interest public, this is referred to as The Veil being Lifted.

The [[United Nations Global Occult Coalition|Global Occult Coalition]] also maintains the Veil, as it is in their interest to do so.
