A Hume is a measurement used to determine the strength and/or amount of reality in a given area. The levels of Hume range from 0 to 100, measured inside two pocket realities containing [[Scranton Reality Anchor|Scranton Reality Anchors]] that are set maintain Hume levels at an arbitrarily low and high level. This done through the [[Kant Counter]]. You will encounter this measurement a lot when tasked with containing or neutralizing reality benders.
