The **Kant Counter** is an instrument developed by the SCP Foundation to detect and quantify the “thickness” or intensity of reality in a given area by measuring units known as [[Hume (Measurement of Reality)|Humes]]. It operates by comparing local reality against two fixed baselines—established via specialized pocket dimensions equipped with [[Scranton Reality Anchor|Scranton Reality Anchors]]—that are set at 0 and 100 [[Hume (Measurement of Reality)|Humes]]. Essentially, the device uses dual portals that connect to these pocket realities, allowing it to derive a differential measurement that reflects how much the local reality deviates from the established norms. Named in honor of the philosopher Immanuel Kant, the Kant Counter plays a critical role in monitoring, researching, and containing reality-bending phenomena and [[Ontokinesis|ontokinetic]] anomalies.
