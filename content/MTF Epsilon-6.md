# About
**MTF Epsilon-6 "Village Idiots"** is a [[Mobile Task Force]] operating under the [[SCP Foundation]]. We specialize in investigation, containment, and clean-up inside rural and sub-urban environments. Although we are flexible; we can be deployed into other environments if no other suitable candidates are available. Epsilon-6 may be sent instead of [[Containment Team|containment teams]] if there is a high chance of escalation.

Epsilon-6 has an offshore headquarters referred to as Mother Base.

> [!info]- Hidden Note
> Officially, we do specialize in investigation, containment, and clean-up inside rural and sub-urban environments. We look like any old regular MTF on the outside. Unofficially though, we are an MTF that consists of members who wouldn't be able to fit into other MTFs. We use unorthodox--and truthfully--idiotic methods to try and get the job done no matter what, while being laxer in attitude than the others. We will be deployed into areas and scenarios outside our jurisdiction as expendables if other MTFs are unavailable. Deal with it.

## Sections
Epsilon-6 has 8 sections, with some being on hold.
### Active Sections
#### Gamma
Infantry specialized in CBRN-E, typically the first ones in.

Specialties:
- Flamethrowers & Incendiaries
- Gas detection + treatment
#### Delta
Infantry specialized in Electronic Warfare.

Specialties:
- Signal tracking
- Cyberwarfare
#### Spectre
Infantry specialized in recon.

Specialties:
- Reconnaissance
- Stealth
- Marksmanship
#### Hound
Mechanized and armored.

Specialties:
- ~~Blowing shit up~~
- Manning ground vehicles
- Operating drones
#### Sigma
Command staff operating on the ground.
### Inactive Sections
#### Nomad/Valkyrie
Aerial section. Nomad for transport, valkyrie for CAS.

Currently inactive as the need for aerial is minimal.
#### Trauma
Infantry specialized in medical, expected to operate on their own.

Currently inactive due to lack of manpower.
